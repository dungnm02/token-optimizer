Presentation Structure and Flow
Slide 1: Start with a strong visual hook: a "$5.00" cost counter ticking up. 
Slide 3 & 4 (Tokens & Whitespace): Include an interactive step-through of the BPE merge process. Show a live comparison of pretty JSON (19 tokens) vs minified JSON (11 tokens). 
Slide 6 (Causes of Leaks): Use an interactive 2x3 grid of cards that expand on click 
Slide 10, 11, 12 (The 3 Tools): Compare "Marketing claims' vs. "Real measured numbers on my machine" for CodeGraph, RTK, and Caveman. Embed actual terminal outputs statically. 
Slide 13 (The Core Diagram): An animated vertical pipeline showing where tools fit: CodeGraph (Static Input) -> RTK (Dynamic Input) -> Prefill/Decode-> Caveman (Output). 
Slide 14 (Tips & Tricks): List 13 actionable tips (e.g., order prompts by volatility, freeze system prompts, minify before sending). 
Slide 15 (When NOT to use): Highlight edge cases (e.g. debugging flaky tests, human-read documents, stale indexes).
Slide 17 (Caching Simulation): A custom animated "payload bar"" demonstrating cold starts, cache hits (-90%), TTL eviction
after a 10-minute coffee break, and the penalty of dynamic data (like logs) placed at the top of a prompt.
